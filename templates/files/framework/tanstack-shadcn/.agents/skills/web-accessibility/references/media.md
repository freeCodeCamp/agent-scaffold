# Audio and Media

Read this reference when building audio players, video players, or media controls.

## Auto-Playing Audio (SC 1.4.2)

If audio starts automatically and lasts more than 3 seconds, provide a mechanism
to pause or stop it, or to reduce its volume independently of the system volume.

## Audio-Only Content (SC 1.2.1)

Recorded audio with no video track (podcasts, audio clips) needs a text
transcript as a synchronized alternative.

## Video With Audio (SC 1.2.2, 1.2.5)

- Prerecorded synchronized media needs captions (closed captions are sufficient;
  open captions are fine too).
- Prerecorded video needs an audio description track, or a version of the video
  that includes the description narrated into the existing audio.

## Live Audio/Video (SC 1.2.4)

Live synchronized media needs live captions.

## Volume and Mute Controls

Volume and mute controls must be keyboard-operable and meet the same target-size
and contrast requirements as any other interactive element. Do not style them as
decorative overlays.
